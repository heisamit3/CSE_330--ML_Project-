Model Dashboard

Overview of the ensemble pipeline, techniques, and model architecture.

DeBERTa-v3-large

Primary — Classification
  • 65-class classification head
  • Focal Loss (γ=2) + class weights
  • AWP + R-Drop + EMA
  • Layerwise LR decay
  • 5-fold × 2-seed × 4-epoch

BGE-Large-en-v1.5

Secondary — Retrieval
  • Bi-encoder architecture
  • MultipleNegativesRankingLoss
  • Enriched label descriptions
  • Cosine similarity → Softmax
  • 5-fold × 3 epochs

Ensemble + Post-Processing

Fusion
  • α × DeBERTa + (1-α) × Retrieval
  • OOF grid-search for α
  • Family filter (True_*/False_*)
  • Temperature scaling
  • MAP@3 optimization

Explainable AI

Optional
  • Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (primary)
  • Flan-T5-large (fallback)
  • 4-bit NF4 quantization
  • Knowledge base of examples
  • Teacher-style feedback

About MAP

Understanding the MAP pipeline for charting student math misunderstandings.

The Challenge

Predict students' math misconceptions from their open-ended explanations. Given a math question, the student's chosen answer (A/B/C/D), and their written explanation, the model must identify what misconception the student has — ranked as a top-3 list.

The evaluation metric is MAP@3 (Mean Average Precision at 3) across 65 unique combined labels spanning 6 categories and 35 misconception types.
